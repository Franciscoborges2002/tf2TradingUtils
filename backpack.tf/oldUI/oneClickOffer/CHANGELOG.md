# Version 1.0.0

- Added a button next to eligible listings that instantly sends the trade offer with the correct currency — no review step
- Sell listings: buys their item, paying with our own currency (uses Steam's "for_item" param to pre-fill the item)
- Buy orders: sells one of our items, matched by name, paid in the partner's currency
- Skips listings that need the other user added as a friend first, and buy orders that can't be safely matched by name alone (generic unusual, or modified with paint/spell/parts/killstreak)
